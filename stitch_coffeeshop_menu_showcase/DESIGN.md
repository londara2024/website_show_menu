## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an elevated artisanal café experience, marrying traditional editorial craftsmanship with understated modern minimalism. Designed exclusively for browse-and-discover menu presentation rather than transactional e-commerce, the interface prioritizes visual calm, rich sensory storytelling, and effortless legibility.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The aesthetic evokes the tranquil, tactile ambiance of a specialty roastery: warm natural light, steamed milk, polished wood surfaces, and the aroma of single-origin beans. Guests should feel welcomed, unhurried, and confident in the premium culinary craft behind each offering.

### Design Movement
A synthesis of **Warm Minimalism** and **Editorial Print Tradition**:
- Uncluttered compositions with deliberate, breathing white space.
- Expressive, high-contrast serif headlines delivering culinary authority.
- Soft, organic geometry via gentle card radiuses and warm, ambient drop shadows.
- Distinct textural separation using creamy tinted surface tiers instead of abrasive structural lines.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from coffee cultivation, roasting tiers, and café architecture. The light-mode palette is calibrated around a soft, unbleached cream foundation to eliminate eye strain while sustaining a cozy, tactile presence.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#6F3E23` / Coffee Roast):** Anchors brand accents, primary buttons, focal active states, and editorial highlights.
- **Secondary (`#3B2116` / Dark Espresso):** High-contrast anchor for typography, structural headers, icon outlines, and dark atmospheric cards.
- **Tertiary (`#C89552` / Warm Honey Crema):** Used selectively for specialty roaster badges, origin tags, seasonal highlights, and subtle rating accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` / Warm Cream):** Default background canvas; warmer and softer than harsh white, establishing an editorial paper quality.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF` / Steamed Milk):** Elevated surface cards, floating item panels, and input containers to lift content off the cream backdrop.
- **Muted Earth (`#DDCFC2` / Café Oat):** Delicate borders, subtle separators, and disabled element backgrounds.
- **Subtle Text (`#705E54` / Roasted Husk):** Secondary supporting copy, tasting notes, and volume specifications.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic coffee house charm and functional modern UI clarity. 

### Roles & Pairings
- **Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Delivers artisanal warmth, high stroke contrast, and timeless beauty. It should always be set with generous line heights and balanced tracking to honor its editorial spirit.
- **Body & Pricing (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its friendly geometry, legible terminals, and clear vertical metrics. When presenting bean origins, dietary markers, or sizing matrices (e.g., 8oz / 12oz / 16oz), the low-contrast sans ensures crisp scanning without competing with the serifs.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered in uppercase with increased letter spacing (`0.06em` - `0.08em`) to act as refined editorial watermarks across seasonal batches and roast notes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout favors deliberate, uncrowded structures inspired by boutique print publications. It avoids chaotic grids in favor of structured rhythmic flows.

### Grid & Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max content width of `1200px` centered within a fluid 12-column grid. Column gutters span `1.5rem` (`24px`) with outer canvas margins of `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` side margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Complex multi-column price lists flatten gracefully into single-column or stacked card units.

### Spacing Discipline
- **Section Transitions:** Employ `space-xl` to segment primary drink categories (e.g., Pour Over, Espresso Bar, Bakery, Seasonal Specialties).
- **Component Breathing Room:** High internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`) inside cards gives each beverage and pastry adequate prominence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold, neutral dropshadows or stark digital borders. Instead, elevation mirrors soft afternoon sunlight filtering through café storefront windows.

### Depth Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Tone `#FAF7F2` (Warm Cream).
- **Layer 1 (Subtle Cards & Sections):** Tone `#FFFFFF` with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(59, 33, 22, 0.05)`, softened with a delicate border: `1px solid rgba(111, 62, 35, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive & Hovered Cards):** Lifted state with extended diffusion: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(59, 33, 22, 0.09)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Filter Drawers):** `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(59, 33, 22, 0.16)`.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** The presence of warm brown tints in shadow calculations keeps elements rooted to the café surface, preventing clinical grey appearances.

## Shapes

With a roundedness value of `2`, the design system relies on a calibrated `0.5rem` (`8px`) baseline corner radius. This balances the structure of traditional print paper with the soft, comforting curvature of ceramic cups and stone counter edges.

### Radius Scale
- **Base Elements (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Buttons, selection chips, small thumbnails, allergen indicators.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 1.0rem / 16px):** Drink catalog cards, origin feature showcases, menu category panels.
- **Hero & Modal Sheets (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Signature hero highlight panels, full category banners, bottom drawer sheets on mobile.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Used exclusively for compact status tags, roast level badges (e.g., "Light Roast", "Direct Trade"), and category scroll tabs.

## Components

### Menu Cards
- **Architecture:** Pure display cards featuring high-contrast layout separation: beverage name in `Playfair Display`, tasting notes/origin in `body-sm`, and pricing tables aligned cleanly on the right or bottom.
- **Background & Border:** White surface `#FFFFFF` with a `1px` border of `rgba(111, 62, 35, 0.08)` and `rounded-lg`.
- **Hover Behavior:** Translates `-2px` on the Y-axis accompanied by Layer 2 shadow expansion; the item title transitions to the primary color `#6F3E23`.

### Multi-Size Pricing Layouts
- **Structure:** Clean sans-serif grid or inline flex presentation showing size indicators (`8oz`, `12oz`, `16oz` or `Single`, `Double`) positioned directly above or beside corresponding prices.
- **Styling:** Sizes labeled in `label-caps` with `#705E54`; prices displayed in `price-lg` with `#3B2116`. Subtle dotted or hairline rules connect item descriptions to pricing rows.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Category Filter:** Horizontal scrollable rail of pill badges (`9999px`).
- **Default State:** Transparent or soft cream background with `#705E54` text and `1px solid rgba(111, 62, 35, 0.15)`.
- **Active State:** `#6F3E23` background with pure white `#FFFFFF` text and subtle warm drop shadow.

### Buttons (Exploration & Navigation)
- **Primary:** Deep roasted coffee `#6F3E23` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, bold geometric sans typography. Hover darkens to `#3B2116`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#6F3E23`, text in `#6F3E23`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(111, 62, 35, 0.05)`.

### Badges & Roast Indicators
- **Style:** Compact pill tags (`px: 8px, py: 4px`) styled in `label-badge`.
- **Origin / Organic:** Tertiary `#C89552` background with `#FAF7F2` or deep espresso text.
- **Dietary Icons:** Minimal circular badges (Vegan, Dairy-Free, Decaf) rendered with `1px` outline in `#DDCFC2` and coffee brown iconography.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Search Bar:** Generous `space-md` height, clean `#FFFFFF` field with `rounded-lg`, framed by a muted earth outline. Placeholder text set in italicized `#705E54` with search iconography in `#6F3E23`. Focus state introduces a crisp `2px` ring in `#6F3E23` at 20% opacity.
